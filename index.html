Subject-Level Validation of Independent Voice, Spiral-Drawing and T1-Weighted MRI Classifiers for Parkinson’s Disease

An audit of three small public cohorts. When repeated recordings, adaptive model selection and acquisition differences are controlled, how much Parkinson’s discrimination is left?

Romana Rahman and Nahid Islam NayemDepartment of Computer Science and Engineering, Khwaja Yunus Ali University, Sirajganj, Bangladesh

Launch live app View code Paper (PDF) Cite

Research use only. Nothing on this page or in the demo app is a medical device or a diagnostic tool. Scores are uncalibrated, and no modality showed discrimination that was clearly different from chance or confounding at these sample sizes.

Method

How the analysis is built

Every preprocessing and selection step is fitted inside training folds only. Click a diagram to enlarge it.

Each cohort is split into a development set and a hold-out. Both the fixed-LR primary analysis and the nested secondary analysis run on the development set only.

Ten random partitions × three outer folds. All tuning happens in the inner CV. The permutation null re-runs the whole procedure on shuffled labels.

SynthStrip → N4 → rigid MNI152 (2 mm) → intensity normalisation, then voxel and MedicalNet branches. Automated QC defines the QC-pass sensitivity set.

Confounding audit

The spiral signal is mostly the recording device

PD and control spirals were recorded with different sampling intervals. Acquisition descriptors alone classify almost perfectly, and the shape signal disappears once acquisition is regressed out.

Controlled experiment

Splitting voice by recording inflates accuracy

Same 22 features, same models, same three-fold structure — only the splitting unit changes. With recording-level folds, the same speakers appear in both training and test sets.

Recording-level, 80/20 Recording-level, 3-fold Subject-level, 3-fold

Shared identifiers between train and test (mean of 10 seeds): 22.8 (80/20), 29.4 (recording 3-fold), 0 (subject 3-fold).

Live demo

Try the research demo app

A Streamlit app that runs the locked voice, spiral and MRI models on your own files. It is meant for reproducing the study, not for diagnosis.

What you can do

  1. Choose a modality. Voice (acoustic-feature CSV), spiral (raw tablet TXT or feature CSV) or T1 MRI (normalised NPY + metadata JSON).
  2. Download the input template and fill it in, or upload a file produced by the notebook.
  3. Get an uncalibrated research score with input validation and the study’s quality flags.
